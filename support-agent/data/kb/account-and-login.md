## Password reset
Customers can reset their password from the login page using "Forgot password". The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. Support can trigger a reset email but cannot see or set passwords.

## Locked accounts
Accounts lock after 5 failed login attempts for 15 minutes. Support cannot unlock early.

## Changing the account email
The customer must request the change from the account settings page while logged in. Support cannot change the email on an account by request over email, to protect against account takeover.

## Deleting an account
Account deletion and personal data requests are handled by the privacy team. Forward these requests to privacy@northwind-outfitters.example; do not delete data from support tools.
