title: Product Care and Sizing
---

## Down jackets
Wash on a gentle cold cycle with down-specific detergent and tumble dry low with dryer balls. Do not dry clean.

## Waterproof shells
Re-apply DWR treatment when water stops beading. Machine wash cold with technical wash, no fabric softener.

## Sizing
Our jackets run true to size with room for a mid-layer. Footwear runs a half size small; we recommend ordering up a half size for wide feet.

## Sleeping bags
Store uncompressed in the included mesh sack. Temperature ratings are comfort ratings tested to EN 13537.
