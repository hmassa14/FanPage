title: Order Changes and Cancellations
---

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled by support any time before they ship. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; the customer should refuse delivery or start a return.

## Changing items
Items cannot be swapped on an existing order. Cancel and reorder if the order has not shipped.

## Price adjustments
If an item's price drops within 7 days of purchase, support may refund the difference as store credit once per order.

## Discount codes
Discount codes cannot be applied after an order is placed.
