title: Shipping and Delivery
---

## Processing time
Orders ship within 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed after 2pm Eastern on Friday ship the following Monday.

## Delivery estimates
Standard shipping arrives in 3 to 7 business days. Expedited shipping arrives in 2 business days. Overnight shipping arrives the next business day when ordered before 12pm Eastern.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show movement after the label is created.

## Late or missing packages
If tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, support opens a carrier trace. If the carrier marks the package delivered but the customer has not received it, ask the customer to check with neighbors and the building office and wait 48 hours; after that, support may ship a replacement or refund the order.

## Damaged in transit
If an item arrives damaged, the customer should send photos within 7 days of delivery. Support may ship a replacement at no charge or issue a full refund. No return of the damaged item is required for items under $75.

## Address changes
The shipping address can be changed only before the order ships. After shipping, address changes must be requested through the carrier and are not guaranteed.
