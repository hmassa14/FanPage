## Coverage
Northwind-branded gear is covered against manufacturing defects for 2 years from the purchase date. Third-party brands sold on our site carry the manufacturer's own warranty.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, improper care, accidents, or alterations are not covered.

## How to file a claim
Email support with the order number, a description of the defect, and photos. Claims are reviewed within 3 business days. Approved claims receive a repair, replacement, or store credit at Northwind's discretion.
