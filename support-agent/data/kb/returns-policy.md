title: Returns and Refunds Policy
---

## Return window
Unused items in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method. Items returned between 31 and 60 days after delivery are eligible for store credit only. After 60 days, returns are not accepted.

## Condition requirements
Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have all tags attached. Footwear must be returned in the original shoebox. Items showing wear or damage caused by the customer are not eligible for refund.

## Non-returnable items
Final-sale items (marked "Final Sale" on the product page), gift cards, and opened food or fuel canisters cannot be returned.

## Refund processing
Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the warehouse receives and inspects the return. Banks may take an additional 3 to 10 business days to post the credit.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for customers in the United States using the prepaid label from the returns portal. International customers pay return shipping unless the item arrived defective.

## Refund authority
Support agents may issue refunds up to $50 without manager approval when policy conditions are met. Refunds above $50 require a support lead to approve. Goodwill credits (outside policy) require a support lead at any amount.
